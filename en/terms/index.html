Fovebo General Terms

General contractual terms for B2B engineering services

July 10, 2026 · English

This page contains Fovebo's general contractual terms for B2B engineering services. Client-specific individual agreements, offers, Statements of Work, service descriptions and separately incorporated documents may apply in addition and take precedence in case of express deviation.

The English version is provided for convenience and website consistency. Unless expressly agreed otherwise in a client-specific individual agreement, the German version governs.

Fovebo General Terms

General contractual terms for B2B engineering services

1. Scope and incorporation

(1) These Fovebo General Terms apply to all B2B services of Fovebo LLC ("Fovebo") to entrepreneurs, legal entities under public law or special funds under public law ("Client"), to the extent they are incorporated into an offer, individual agreement, service description or other agreement.

(2) These Terms do not apply to consumers.

(3) Individual agreements, in particular a signed offer, Statement of Work, client-specific service description or other expressly agreed provisions, take precedence over these Terms.

(4) General terms and conditions, purchasing terms or other contractual terms of the Client apply only if Fovebo expressly agrees to their application in text form. Silence, service performance or acceptance of payments does not constitute consent.

(5) Fovebo may use different offer formats, in particular flexible time-based services, capacity retainers, workshops, audits, packaged services, sprints or project-based implementations. The specific offer format is determined by the relevant individual agreement.

2. Contract conclusion and contract components

(1) A contract is concluded by signature, electronic signature, express acceptance of an offer in text form or by commencement of service performance after mutual coordination.

(2) The specific scope of services, remuneration, payment method, term, notice periods, capacity, dates, contacts, communication channels, access and other project-specific details are determined by the relevant individual agreement.

(3) Where required, in particular a confidentiality agreement, a data processing agreement, standard contractual clauses, a Security Baseline, Data Access Boundaries or offboarding documents may be incorporated separately.

(4) In case of contradictions, the following order of precedence applies: (a) individually negotiated agreements, (b) offer, Statement of Work or service description, (c) a separately concluded confidentiality agreement for confidentiality matters, (d) data protection or third-country transfer agreements for data protection matters, (e) these General Terms and (f) further annexes, unless expressly agreed otherwise.

3. Service model

(1) Fovebo provides services in particular in the areas of software development, backend engineering, databases, PostgreSQL performance, technical analysis, modernization, maintenance, consulting, code review, automation, tooling, documentation and related technical disciplines.

(2) The services are generally provided as services. Fovebo owes the professional performance of the agreed activities, but not a specific technical or economic result, unless the individual agreement expressly provides for a specific result, acceptance or a deviating contract-for-work arrangement.

(3) Packaged services may include defined activities, results and deliverables. The provision of a report, analysis, recommendation, code artifact or documentation does not by itself establish any guarantee of a specific performance improvement, freedom from errors, availability or economic effect.

(4) Tickets, backlogs, pull requests, repositories, documentation systems, time tracking, meetings or sprints serve coordination, transparency and collaboration. They do not establish organizational integration of Fovebo into the Client's operations.

(5) Fovebo does not owe product responsibility, project management, on-call duty, 24/7 availability, client representation or other tasks outside the expressly agreed scope of services.

4. Remote work, place of work and collaboration

(1) Fovebo generally provides services remotely from Paraguay. The Client is aware that service performance does not take place physically within the EU or the EEA unless expressly agreed otherwise.

(2) Collaboration is generally remote and async-first. Alignment, questions and coordination take place through the agreed communication channels and, where appropriate, within agreed overlap windows with the Client's teams.

(3) An agreed overlap window serves collaboration and coordination. It does not establish a duty of permanent availability, employee-like working time, on-call duty or a presence obligation.

(4) If the Client has internal policies requiring physical work performance within the EU or the EEA or at a specific location, the Client must communicate this before contract conclusion. If such a requirement is mandatory, collaboration may not be possible or may be possible only with a different structure.

5. Independence and no employee leasing

(1) The parties act as legally independent companies. These Terms and the relevant individual agreement do not establish an employment relationship, partnership, commercial agency relationship or other representation relationship.

(2) Fovebo provides the services on its own responsibility and is not organizationally integrated into the Client's operations. Fovebo independently determines place of work, work organization, approach, methods and internal service performance.

(3) Requirements of the Client are limited to technical requirements, business priorities, appropriate security requirements, cooperation, approvals and scheduling coordination within the agreed scope of services. Employment-law instructions do not exist.

(4) The parties agree that no employee leasing is intended. No employees are leased to the Client.

(5) The Client has no claim to the deployment of specific persons unless this is expressly set out in the individual agreement.

6. No authority to represent and no client representation

(1) Fovebo is not authorized to legally represent the Client, conclude contracts in the Client's name, promise prices or commercial terms to third parties or otherwise legally bind the Client.

(2) Communication with customers, suppliers or other third parties of the Client takes place only to the extent required for technical coordination or expressly coordinated. This does not include commercial or legal representation.

(3) Fovebo will not create the impression toward third parties that it is an employee, corporate body, commercial agent or legal representative of the Client.

7. Client cooperation obligations

(1) The Client provides all information, documents, decisions, contacts, access, roles, permissions, test data, documentation, approvals and other prerequisites required for service performance in good time.

(2) Cooperation requests from Fovebo must be processed within a reasonably prompt time. To the extent cooperation acts are not performed in time, dates, priorities and schedules shift accordingly; further rights of Fovebo remain unaffected.

(3) Security, data protection, compliance, purchasing or other internal requirements of the Client must be communicated to Fovebo in text form before the affected service begins. They apply only to the extent they are reasonable, proportionate and compatible with the agreed scope of services.

(4) Delays, additional effort or performance obstacles caused by missing, late or withdrawn cooperation by the Client are not attributable to Fovebo and do not create any automatic obligation to reperform or transfer capacity free of charge.

(5) The Client remains responsible for appropriate safeguarding of its systems and data, in particular for regular backups and suitable recovery options, unless corresponding services have been expressly transferred to Fovebo.

8. Work equipment, client systems and access

(1) Fovebo generally uses its own professional work equipment unless the Client requires specific work equipment or client devices for security, compliance or organizational reasons.

(2) If the Client requires the use of specific work equipment, in particular a secured device, the Client provides it in operational condition and is responsible for provisioning, access, updates, support and return.

(3) Provided work equipment remains the property of the Client. Fovebo uses it exclusively for contract performance and returns it after contract end or upon request.

(4) Access to client systems may be used exclusively for service performance. The Client remains responsible for provision, technical functionality, role and rights management and revocation of access.

9. Access, data and security model

(1) Access, permissions and data should be structured according to the principle of least privilege ("Least Privilege").

(2) Where technically possible, analysis, development, testing and troubleshooting are performed on the basis of test, staging, anonymized, pseudonymized or otherwise risk-reduced data.

(3) Access to production systems or personal production data occurs only to the extent required for service performance, approved by the Client and traceable.

(4) Fovebo does not create local copies of personal or otherwise confidential client data unless this is required for service performance or expressly approved.

(5) Client data no longer required must be deleted or returned after contract end or upon request in accordance with contractual and statutory requirements.

(6) Supplementary technical and organizational measures or project-specific access boundaries may be agreed separately where needed.

(7) If additional security or compliance requirements of the Client lead to significant additional effort, the parties will agree on an appropriate adjustment of dates, capacity or remuneration.

10. Confidentiality

(1) The parties undertake not to disclose confidential information of the other party to third parties and to use it exclusively for contractual purposes.

(2) Confidential information means all information marked as confidential or confidential by its nature, in particular trade secrets, technical information, source code, access credentials, security information, client data, prices, strategies, internal documentation and non-public project information.

(3) The confidentiality obligation does not apply to information that is publicly known, becomes publicly known without breach of contract, was already lawfully known to the receiving party, was lawfully disclosed by a third party or was independently developed without use of confidential information.

(4) The confidentiality obligation continues after contract end.

(5) To the extent the parties conclude a separate confidentiality agreement, that agreement takes precedence over these General Terms for confidentiality matters.

11. Data protection and third-country access

(1) The parties clarify on a project-specific basis whether and to what extent personal data is processed and which data protection roles apply.

(2) If Fovebo processes personal data on behalf of the Client, the parties will conclude a data processing agreement (DPA) before the relevant processing begins or use a suitable client template.

(3) To the extent service performance involves access from a third country outside the EU or the EEA and appropriate safeguards are required for this, the parties will separately agree the necessary additional arrangements, in particular standard contractual clauses or comparable transfer documentation.

(4) Fovebo reports data protection or security incidents that may affect personal data or confidential information of the Client to the Client without undue delay after becoming aware of them.

(5) The parties cooperate to a reasonable extent in containing, investigating, documenting and remediating such incidents.

12. Subcontractors and other third parties

(1) Fovebo may use suitable subcontractors or other third parties for service performance. To the extent they are to provide project-related services and receive access to confidential information, client systems or client data, Fovebo informs the Client before their use in text form.

(2) The Client may object to their use within five business days for legitimate security, confidentiality or data protection reasons. In this case, the parties agree on an appropriate alternative approach.

(3) Fovebo remains responsible for contractual service performance and compliance with the obligations it has assumed.

(4) General service providers of Fovebo that do not provide project-related services and do not receive access to client systems or client data are not considered project-related subcontractors. Data protection requirements for subprocessors remain unaffected.

13. Rights to work results and pre-existing rights

(1) For changes and additions to the Client's own software as well as source code, configurations, database schemas and integration code that Fovebo creates specifically and exclusively for the Client, the Client receives, after full payment, an exclusive, temporally and geographically unrestricted right of use, to the extent legally transferable and unless otherwise agreed.

(2) Pre-existing or independently developed tools, libraries, templates, generic components, routines, frameworks, scripts, documentation building blocks, working methods and other know-how of Fovebo remain the property or rights portfolio of Fovebo.

(3) To the extent components under paragraph 2 are integrated into a client-specific work result, the Client receives a simple, temporally and geographically unrestricted right of use to them to the extent required for contractual use, maintenance and further development of the work result.

(4) For audit reports, analyses, recommendations, checklists, presentations and comparable consulting or documentation results, the Client receives, after full payment, a simple, temporally and geographically unrestricted right of use for its own internal business purposes. Publication, resale or other external exploitation requires Fovebo's consent unless the individual agreement provides otherwise.

(5) Fovebo may continue to use general know-how, methods, experience, concepts and solution approaches for other clients, provided no confidential information of the Client is disclosed and no client-specific work results are used without authorization.

(6) Open-source software and other third-party components are subject exclusively to their respective license terms. Fovebo is not obligated to transfer rights to such third-party components.

(7) Until full payment of the respectively due remuneration, the Client receives only a provisional, revocable right of use to work results within the scope of the contractually intended use.

14. Remuneration, invoicing and payment

(1) Remuneration, currency, billing cycle, payment term, retainer capacity, hourly rates, daily rates, package prices or other fees are determined by the relevant individual agreement.

(2) All amounts are, where applicable, exclusive of statutory VAT or comparable charges.

(3) Payments must be made without deduction of bank fees, withholding taxes or other withholdings unless such deduction is legally mandatory. If a statutory withholding is required, the Client informs Fovebo in advance and provides suitable evidence of the withholding.

(4) The parties support each other to a reasonable extent in providing required tax certificates, company evidence or forms. VAT treatment, in particular Reverse Charge or comparable procedures, is governed by the respectively applicable tax rules.

(5) Pre-approved expenses and third-party costs are invoiced separately unless they are expressly included in the agreed remuneration.

(6) Additional services outside the agreed scope are performed only after prior coordination and approval in text form and are compensated according to the agreed rates or a separate agreement.

15. Time-based services, retainers and capacity

(1) In the case of flexible time-based billing, no capacity reservation is made unless the individual agreement expressly provides otherwise. Services are performed according to actual use and subject to Fovebo's availability.

(2) Where a retainer or expressly reserved hours or days contingent is agreed, it serves as the binding reservation of the agreed capacity of Fovebo.

(3) The retainer remuneration is owed regardless of whether the Client fully calls off the reserved capacity. Capacity not used within the agreed reference period expires and is not carried over unless the individual agreement provides otherwise.

(4) Fovebo is not obligated to provide more than the agreed or reserved capacity. Additional services require prior coordination and are treated as additional services.

(5) Pauses, minimum terms, deviating carry-over rules and other special rules are determined by the relevant individual agreement.

16. Changes, priorities and change requests

(1) For time-based services, tasks and priorities within the agreed service area may be adjusted on an ongoing basis. Such adjustments consume the available time or capacity, but do not automatically constitute a separate change request.

(2) For packaged, fixed-price or otherwise clearly delineated services, additional requirements, new systems, additional deliverables, extended security or compliance reviews, changed access routes or special documentation obligations may constitute a change request.

(3) Change requests require coordination in text form. If they lead to additional effort, delay or changed risk, dates, capacity, priorities and remuneration may be adjusted appropriately.

(4) Fovebo is not obligated to implement a change request before corresponding approval.

17. Dates, delays and force majeure

(1) Dates and schedules are binding only if expressly agreed as binding. For services, schedules are regularly a planning and coordination basis.

(2) Delays due to missing cooperation, pending approvals, non-functional client systems, changed requirements or force majeure are not attributable to Fovebo.

(3) Force majeure includes in particular natural disasters, war, terrorism, pandemics, governmental orders, prolonged large-scale power, internet or infrastructure outages and other unforeseeable circumstances outside the affected party's sphere of influence.

(4) If a force majeure event lasts longer than thirty days, both parties are entitled to terminate the affected individual agreement for good cause.

18. Payment default and suspension of services

(1) If the Client is in default with a due invoice, Fovebo is entitled, after reminder and expiry of a grace period of five business days, to suspend service performance until full receipt of payment.

(2) A suspension does not affect the term, capacity reservation or remuneration obligation of the affected individual agreement.

(3) In case of payment default, statutory default interest and default lump sums apply where applicable.

(4) If the Client remains in default for longer than thirty calendar days or payment default occurs repeatedly, Fovebo may terminate the affected individual agreement extraordinarily for good cause.

19. Term, termination and contract end

(1) Term, minimum term and ordinary termination are determined by the relevant individual agreement.

(2) The right to extraordinary termination for good cause remains unaffected.

(3) At contract end, outstanding remuneration, approved additional services, approved expenses and other accrued claims must be invoiced.

(4) Access, client data, work equipment, secrets and open work results are handled in accordance with the individual agreement, these Terms and any separately incorporated documents.

(5) Support services as part of extended offboarding, migration or handover to third parties are compensated on a time basis unless they are already part of the individual agreement.

20. Liability

(1) Fovebo is liable in accordance with statutory provisions for intent and gross negligence as well as for damages arising from injury to life, body or health.

(2) In case of slightly negligent breach of material contractual obligations, liability is limited to the typical, foreseeable damage. For one-off, packaged or project-related individual agreements, the liability cap corresponds to the net remuneration of the affected individual agreement; for ongoing agreements, it corresponds to the net remuneration that was paid or owed in the three months before the event giving rise to liability. In any case, the liability cap is at least EUR 10,000 and at most EUR 50,000.

(3) Otherwise, Fovebo's liability for slight negligence is excluded. Liability for lost profit, unrealized savings and indirect consequential damages is excluded to the extent legally permissible.

(4) In case of loss or damage to data, Fovebo is liable only for the restoration effort that would have arisen with proper and regular data backup by the Client, unless data backup expressly belongs to Fovebo's scope of services.

(5) Fovebo is not liable for outages, changes or restrictions of third-party systems, hosting services, platforms, open-source components or other infrastructure outside Fovebo's sphere of influence, to the extent Fovebo is not responsible for its own breach of duty in selection, integration or use.

(6) Mandatory statutory liability, in particular under product liability law or for fraudulently concealed defects, remains unaffected.

21. Reference use

(1) Fovebo may identify the Client by name and include a short, factual and non-confidential description of the general type of service as a reference after the Client has been informed of this in text form. The Client may object to the reference within ten business days for legitimate reasons.

(2) Use of the Client logo, publication of a detailed case study, specific results, internal metrics or non-public project information requires the Client's prior consent in text form.

(3) Confidential information, security details, personal data and other non-public information of the Client may not be disclosed as part of reference use.

22. Text form, electronic communication and signatures

(1) Where text form is agreed, this includes in particular email, electronic signature, written confirmation in an agreed tool or another permanently readable electronic declaration, provided the sender is identifiable.

(2) Electronic signatures and signing in several counterparts are permitted to the extent legally permissible.

(3) Operational coordination in tickets, pull requests, emails or chat systems may count as text form to the extent the parties use these channels for the project and the content is sufficiently clearly documented.

23. Final provisions

(1) The law of the Federal Republic of Germany applies, excluding the UN Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods (CISG).

(2) Place of jurisdiction is the place of jurisdiction specified in the relevant individual agreement, to the extent legally permissible. If no provision is made, the statutory places of jurisdiction apply.

(3) If a provision of these Terms or an individual agreement is or becomes invalid, the validity of the remaining provisions remains unaffected. The invalid provision is replaced by the statutory provisions.

(4) Fovebo may amend these General Terms for future contracts. For existing individual agreements, the version incorporated into that agreement applies unless the parties agree on a new version.
